# Indian Railways Zones and Divisions Reference

This document provides a comprehensive list of all **19 operational zones** and their corresponding **divisions** within the Indian Railways, reflecting recent administrative restructurings (such as the formation of the **South Coast Railway (SCoR)** and the **Rayagada Division** under East Coast Railway).

---

## 1. Central Railway (CR)
*   **Headquarters:** Mumbai CSMT
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Mumbai CSMT
    2.  Bhusawal
    3.  Pune
    4.  Solapur
    5.  Nagpur

## 2. Eastern Railway (ER)
*   **Headquarters:** Fairlie Place, Kolkata
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Howrah
    2.  Sealdah
    3.  Asansol
    4.  Malda

## 3. East Central Railway (ECR)
*   **Headquarters:** Hajipur
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Danapur
    2.  Dhanbad
    3.  Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhyaya (formerly Mughalsarai)
    4.  Samastipur
    5.  Sonpur

## 4. East Coast Railway (ECoR)
*   **Headquarters:** Bhubaneswar
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Khurda Road
    2.  Sambalpur
    3.  Rayagada (newly formed after the bifurcation of Waltair Division)

## 5. Northern Railway (NR)
*   **Headquarters:** Baroda House, New Delhi
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Delhi
    2.  Firozpur
    3.  Lucknow NR
    4.  Moradabad
    5.  Ambala

## 6. North Central Railway (NCR)
*   **Headquarters:** Prayagraj (formerly Allahabad)
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Prayagraj
    2.  Agra
    3.  Jhansi

## 7. North Eastern Railway (NER)
*   **Headquarters:** Gorakhpur
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Izzatnagar
    2.  Lucknow NER
    3.  Varanasi

## 8. Northeast Frontier Railway (NFR)
*   **Headquarters:** Maligaon, Guwahati
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Katihar
    2.  Alipurduar
    3.  Lumding
    4.  Rangiya
    5.  Tinsukia

## 9. North Western Railway (NWR)
*   **Headquarters:** Jaipur
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Jaipur
    2.  Ajmer
    3.  Bikaner
    4.  Jodhpur

## 10. Southern Railway (SR)
*   **Headquarters:** Chennai Central, Chennai
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Chennai
    2.  Madurai
    3.  Tiruchchirappalli
    4.  Palakkad
    5.  Thiruvananthapuram
    6.  Salem

## 11. South Central Railway (SCR)
*   **Headquarters:** Secunderabad
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Secunderabad
    2.  Hyderabad
    3.  Nanded
    *(Note: Guntakal, Guntur, and Vijayawada divisions were transferred to the new South Coast Railway zone)*

## 12. South Coast Railway (SCoR)
*   **Headquarters:** Visakhapatnam
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Vijayawada
    2.  Guntakal
    3.  Guntur
    4.  Visakhapatnam (bifurcated from Waltair Division)

## 13. South Eastern Railway (SER)
*   **Headquarters:** Garden Reach, Kolkata
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Kharagpur
    2.  Adra
    3.  Chakradharpur
    4.  Ranchi

## 14. South East Central Railway (SECR)
*   **Headquarters:** Bilaspur
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Bilaspur
    2.  Raipur
    3.  Nagpur SECR

## 15. South Western Railway (SWR)
*   **Headquarters:** Hubballi (formerly Hubli)
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Hubballi
    2.  Bengaluru
    3.  Mysuru

## 16. Western Railway (WR)
*   **Headquarters:** Churchgate, Mumbai
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Mumbai Central
    2.  Vadodara
    3.  Ahmedabad
    4.  Ratlam
    5.  Rajkot
    6.  Bhavnagar

## 17. West Central Railway (WCR)
*   **Headquarters:** Jabalpur
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Jabalpur
    2.  Bhopal
    3.  Kota

## 18. Metro Railway, Kolkata
*   **Headquarters:** Kolkata
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Kolkata Metro (administered directly as a distinct zonal railway entity)

## 19. Konkan Railway (KR)
*   **Headquarters:** Navi Mumbai
*   **Divisions:**
    1.  Ratnagiri
    2.  Karwar
    *(Note: Administered by the Konkan Railway Corporation Limited (KRCL) under the Ministry of Railways)*
